# CHR-001 背视图 · 参考图提示词 v1

以已确认的 `a-front.png` 为输入图（唯一造型依据），补画背面。配件左右以正视图为准（皮袋在角色右胯、铜环在左侧）：从背后看，皮袋在画面右侧、铜环在画面左侧。两张侧视图左右互相矛盾，不作为生成输入。

Seen directly from behind at eye level, so the small leather pouch on the character's right hip appears on the right side of the image and the copper ring on the character's left hip appears on the left side of the image. Show how the leather straps cross on the back and how the cape and hood cover the back. Same premium stylized 3D art style, colors and materials as the attached image, full body from hood tip to boots, single character, centered, neutral light gray background, soft even studio lighting, no text, no watermark.
